# Academic Stress, Motivation, and Exercise Behavior

This synthetic dataset contains measures of academic stress, motivation,
exercise intention, and exercise behavior. It was generated based on the
real data by Zhao and colleagues (2025), who conducted research among
290 middle-school students.

## Format

A tibble with 291 observations and 31 variables:

- ID:

  Participant identifier.

- Grade:

  School grade, coded 1 = Grade 7, 2 = Grade 8, and 3 = Grade 9.

- Age:

  Age in years.

- Gender:

  Gender, coded 1 = male and 2 = female.

- TS1-TS4:

  Four items measuring teacher pressure (teacher-related academic
  stress).

- SOS1-SOS4:

  Four items measuring social pressure or social stress.

- PS1-PS5:

  Five items measuring parental pressure or parental stress.

- AM1-AM5:

  Five items measuring autonomous motivation.

- CM1-CM4:

  Four items measuring controlled motivation.

- BI1-BI3:

  Three items measuring behavioral intention to engage in physical
  exercise.

- BH1-BH2:

  Two items measuring physical exercise behavior.

## Source

Zhao, K., Zhao, Y., & Xu, W. (2025). Relationship between middle school
students' academic stress and physical exercise behavior from the
perspective of Self-Determination Theory: The chained mediation of
motivation and intention. *PLOS ONE, 20*(1), e0316599.
[doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0316599](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0316599)

## Details

The dataset contains measures related to Self-Determination Theory (SDT)
in the context of academic stress and physical exercise among
middle-school students.

The stress measures include teacher pressure (TS), social pressure
(SOS), and parental pressure (PS). Motivation is represented by
autonomous motivation (AM) and controlled motivation (CM).
Exercise-related variables include behavioral intention (BI) and
exercise behavior (BH).
